How to use this booklet with the exam note

  • If a question in the exam looks like one of these, find the closest past question here and adapt the answer to the new scenario. The thumb index on the right goes 22, 23, 24, 25.
  • The answers use the same wording as the note, so the two documents agree. For the theory behind a pattern, open that pattern's tab in the note.
  • Each answer is sized to its marks: a 2-mark part gets a name and a reason; a 10-mark part gets a full diagram.
  • Grey boxes hold the question as it was printed. The black tag is the part number and the right side shows the marks.
